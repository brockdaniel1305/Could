# 007 · Could the Roman Empire stop prime Lionel Messi?

Status: ✅ approved by the creator on 2026-10-29 with one change: scene 7 now ends right after the Romans fall ("This is when they all realised that nobody could stop Messi.").
Estimated length: ~110 s (Kokoro preview speed 1.08). Format: present both sides → standoff → the dribble → Rome strikes back → verdict.
Score bug the whole time: **ROME 0 : 0 MESSI** (one point per "round" won).

---

## 1 · Hook (~8 s)
**Narrator:**
> Could the Roman Empire stop prime Lionel Messi?
> We reconstructed the battle. Accurately. … On grass.

**Animation:** A grass battlefield at dawn with the Colosseum in the background. On the right, the front ranks of a Roman legion reach to the horizon. On the left stands little Messi with a ball under his foot. On "Accurately" a card reads **METHOD: Historically accurate**. On "On grass" a second card reads **SURFACE: Grass**, and the chalk lines of a football pitch draw themselves under the army.

---

## 2 · The Roman Empire (~16 s)
**Narrator:**
> In the red corner: the Roman Empire.
> At its peak: five million square kilometres.
> About three hundred and eighty thousand soldiers.
> Shields. Armour. Swords. And centuries of practice.
> They conquered everything from Britain to Egypt. … Nobody ever asked them to defend a football.

**Animation:** The camera pans along the army: row after row of legionaries with red shields, helmets with crests and an SPQR banner. A map card shows the empire in 117 AD growing over Europe. On the soldier count a counter rattles up from 0 to **380,000**. On "Shields. Armour. Swords." the front rank stamps **three times in sync** (a dust puff and a thud each time). On "football" a ball rolls in front of the first legionary. He looks at it, prods it carefully with his spear, and it rolls away.

---

## 3 · Prime Messi (~17 s)
**Narrator:**
> In the blue corner: Lionel Messi.
> One point seven zero metres.
> Eight Ballon d'Ors.
> In twenty twelve, he scored ninety-one goals in one calendar year. A world record.
> In his prime, he was unstoppable. … Defenders did not stop him. They watched him.

**Animation:** A spotlight on Messi in a plain blue-and-red striped shirt with number 10 (no badge, a caricature). On "1.70 m" a height ruler appears next to him, and he really is small. On "Ballon d'Ors" eight golden balls fall one after another into a pile next to him. On "ninety-one" a goal counter rattles up **0 → 91** while little balls fly into a goal at the edge of the frame, getting faster and faster. A **GOAT** card flies in, and a small goat really does wander across the picture. On "They watched him" three cartoon defenders appear and simply turn their heads as he dribbles past.

---

## 4 · The standoff (~13 s)
**Narrator:**
> The battle.
> Three hundred and eighty thousand Romans form a line.
> Messi is alone.
> The odds: three hundred and eighty thousand to one. … Nobody moves. … Messi checks his laces.

**Animation:** Western-style tension. The legion closes into a wall of shields, and Messi stands on the other side. A card reads **ODDS: 380,000 : 1**. A cut to an extreme close-up of a legionary's eyes, then of Messi's eyes. A tumbleweed (a rolled-up Roman helmet plume) blows across the pitch. The wind howls and a drop of sweat runs down the centurion's face. On "laces" Messi bends down, ties his shoe calmly and stands up again. The whole army leans forward a little out of curiosity.

---

## 5 · The dribble (~20 s)
**Narrator:**
> Then he starts dribbling.
> In two thousand seven, against Getafe, he went past five players and the goalkeeper in about twelve seconds.
> Every Roman he passes spins around and gets dizzy.
> At that rate, getting past the whole army takes about nine days.
> Luckily, dizzy Romans fall over. … Into other Romans.

**Animation (the core of the episode):** Messi starts with tiny, quick touches, and the ball sticks to his left foot. He goes past legionaries one at a time with real moves: a step-over, a feint left and go right, and a ball through the legs. Each beaten Roman **spins around his own axis** with little stars or birds circling his head, staggers and stays standing, dizzy. A **DIZZY ROMANS** counter ticks up. On "nine days" a card reads **TIME NEEDED: ~9 days**, and a sun/moon clock in the background races through several days. On "fall over" a dizzy Roman topples against his neighbour and a **domino chain** runs through the rows of the legion. The counter shoots up to the hundreds of thousands. Score: **ROME 0 : 1 MESSI**.

---

## 6 · Rome strikes back (~20 s)
**Narrator:**
> Rome strikes back.
> Plan A: the testudo. A tortoise made of shields.
> Unfortunately, it has legs. … Nutmeg.
> Plan B: what Romans do best. They build a wall. Hadrian's Wall. One hundred and seventeen kilometres long.
> Messi has scored more than sixty-five free kicks over walls. … This one is just longer.

**Animation:** The remaining Romans click into a **testudo** with shields above, in front and at the sides, a closed shell on many legs that shuffles towards Messi. On "it has legs" the camera drops to ground level: under the shell there are 40 legs, and Messi plays the ball through them. The testudo turns around confused and falls apart (**ROME 0 : 2 MESSI**). On "wall" Hadrian's Wall shoots up out of the ground and reaches to the horizon. A **117 km** card appears, and a referee measures out 9.15 m. Messi takes a run-up and curls a free kick over the wall in a high arc (dotted flight path) into the top corner of a goal behind it. A **FREE-KICK GOALS: 65+** card appears. On "just longer" the camera follows the wall to the horizon. **ROME 0 : 3 MESSI**.

---

## 7 · The end (~6 s)
**Narrator:**
> This is when they all realised that nobody could stop Messi.

**Animation:** Every Roman is lying on the ground with stars circling their heads. Messi stands among them with his foot on the ball and his arms crossed. The last three Romans still standing stare at him with their mouths open and drop their swords, and a white flag goes up. A golden **UNSTOPPABLE.** stamp lands on screen. Score **ROME 0 : 3 MESSI**.

**Animation:** Messi walks off juggling the ball. Behind him the Romans are still spinning on the spot. NEXT CASE banner, plus the card **WRITE IT IN THE COMMENTS ↓ ↓ ↓**.

## Facts check (sources in FACTS.md)
- 91 goals in calendar year 2012 (79 Barcelona + 12 Argentina), Guinness World Record.
- 8 Ballon d'Ors, height 1.70 m.
- Roman army under Hadrian: ~380,000 soldiers (Holder 2003: 154,000 legionaries + 223,000 auxiliaries). Empire in 117 AD: ~5 million km².
- Getafe, 18 April 2007: five players plus the goalkeeper, ~60 m, 10–13 s depending on the source → "about twelve seconds".
- Calculation: 6 opponents / 12 s = 0.5 per second → 380,000 / 0.5 = 760,000 s ≈ 8.8 days → "about nine days".
- Hadrian's Wall: 73 Roman miles ≈ 117 km. Free-kick goals: 65+ (varies by source, ~70 as of 2025/26 → "more than sixty-five").
- Lost finals with Argentina: 2014 World Cup, 2015 and 2016 Copa América. World Cup winner 2022.
