# 008 · Could Kylian Mbappé dictate the French Revolution?

Status: 📝 script, waiting for the creator's OK (no build before that).
Estimated length: ~105 s. Format: present both sides → three plans (escalating) → short punchline → outro.
On screen throughout: a **POPULARITY** meter (thumbs down … thumbs up) at the bottom and a calendar sheet at the top right ("14 JULY 1789"), which advances.
Lots of sound effects (crowd, drums, horses, bells, whistle, fanfare).

---

## 1 · Hook (~8 s)
**Narrator:**
> Could Kylian Mbappé dictate the French Revolution?
> We went back to seventeen eighty-nine. … Scientifically. … In French.

**Animation:** Paris in 1789. Notre-Dame and rooftops in the background, an angry crowd with pitchforks, the Bastille on the right. Mbappé drops into the picture out of a swirling time tunnel, lands, and crosses his arms (his celebration). On "In French" a card reads **LANGUAGE: Français**, and a beret drops onto the narrator card.

---

## 2 · France, 1789 (~16 s)
**Narrator:**
> Paris, July seventeen eighty-nine.
> Twenty-eight million French people.
> Bread costs eighty-eight percent of a worker's wage.
> The king, Louis the Sixteenth, is not listening.
> The mood: revolutionary. … The job of leader: vacant.

**Animation:** A population counter rattles up to **28,000,000**. A baker puts a loaf on a scale against a worker's coins; the loaf is heavier and the bar fills to **88 %**. On "not listening" the king appears on the palace balcony in a powdered wig, eating cake and holding a hand to his ear. The crowd waves pitchforks and the noise rises. On "vacant" a sign drops onto the balcony: **WANTED: LEADER**.

---

## 3 · The candidate (~16 s)
**Narrator:**
> The candidate: Kylian Mbappé.
> World champion at nineteen.
> Top speed: about thirty-eight kilometres per hour.
> Captain of France.
> His celebration: arms crossed. Like a man who has already decided. … He usually takes several years to decide.

**Animation:** A spotlight on Mbappé (plain navy kit, number 10, no badge, a caricature). A golden World Cup trophy pops up next to him with a **AGE 19** card. A speedometer card rattles to **38 km/h** while he does one sprint across the picture and back, leaving a dust trail and the cobbles shaking. A captain's armband snaps onto his arm. On "arms crossed" he crosses his arms and gives a satisfied look. On "several years" a calendar next to him flips through pages fast (2021 … 2022 … 2023 …) while he keeps standing there with his arms crossed.

---

## 4 · Plan A: storm the Bastille (~15 s)
**Narrator:**
> Plan A: storm the Bastille.
> On the fourteenth of July, the crowd needed most of the day.
> Mbappé arrives at thirty-eight kilometres per hour. He is first inside.
> He finds seven prisoners. Four of them were forgers. … Seven prisoners. Seven new fans.

**Animation:** The crowd plods slowly towards the fortress while a sun clock in the sky runs from morning to evening. Mbappé overtakes the whole crowd in a speed blur, the drawbridge slams down, and he dashes through the gate. Inside, seven prisoners sit in a cell. The door opens, they blink in the light, and they cheer. Four of them hand him autographs signed with his own name (forged). Popularity meter: ↑ green. Calendar: 14 JULY 1789.

---

## 5 · Plan B: take power (~17 s)
**Narrator:**
> Plan B: take power.
> He declares himself First Captain of the Republic.
> New law: every citizen celebrates with arms crossed.
> The Revolution really did invent a new calendar, with ten days a week. … Mbappé adds an eleventh. … Rest day.

**Animation:** Mbappé on the balcony (the king is gone) with a sash in blue, white and red, a captain's armband and a little crown that is slightly too big. A proclamation scroll unrolls: **DECREE No. 1: ARMS CROSSED**. Below, row after row of citizens cross their arms in a wave from front to back. A counter runs to **28,000,000 arms crossed**. The calendar at the top right gets 10 columns (Primidi, Duodi … Décadi), then an 11th column labelled **REPOS** slides in. Popularity meter: very green, fanfare.

---

## 6 · Plan C: the contract (~17 s)
**Narrator:**
> There is one problem.
> The Revolution offers him a contract. … For life.
> Mbappé needs time to think.
> His move to Real Madrid took three years of rumours.
> By the time he decides, the job is taken. … Napoleon, seventeen ninety-nine. One point six nine metres. … Not fast. But very decisive.

**Animation:**
- A revolutionary official holds out a contract and a quill. Mbappé takes the pen, holds it over the paper, and freezes.
- The calendar at the top right flips faster and faster (1790 … 1795 … 1799). Leaves fall from a tree, snow falls, the sun rises and sets, and cobwebs grow on Mbappé's elbow.
- Meanwhile Napoleon marches into the picture in a bicorne hat. A height ruler shows **1.69 m** next to Mbappé's **1.78 m**. Napoleon takes the contract out of the official's hand and signs it in a single stroke, with a stamp sound.
- Mbappé is left holding a pen and no paper. Popularity meter: back to the middle.

---

## 7 · The punchline (~8 s)
**Narrator:**
> Could Kylian Mbappé dictate the French Revolution? … Yes. … For about a week. … A ten-day week.

**Animation:** A green **YES.** stamp. On "For about a week" the calendar shows a single week of 10 days, and each day is ticked off with a tick sound. On "A ten-day week" a small stamp **(BRIEFLY)** slams onto the YES. Mbappé shrugs with his arms crossed.

---

## 8 · Outro (~7 s)
**Narrator:**
> Could you imagine another scenario that we should do next? Write it in the comments.

**Animation:** Mbappé sprints away down the Paris street, and Napoleon tries to keep up and falls behind. NEXT CASE banner and the comment card.

## Facts check
- France 1789: ~28 million inhabitants. Bread up to 88 % of a worker's wage in 1789 (Labrousse; Neely, *A Concise History of the French Revolution*).
- Bastille, 14 July 1789: the crowd gathered from the morning, ~2 h of fighting, surrender ~17:30 → "most of the day". Only 7 prisoners, 4 of them forgers.
- Republican calendar (from 1793): 12 months × 30 days, "décades" of 10 days (Primidi … Décadi).
- Napoleon: coup of 18 Brumaire, November 1799; height ~1.69 m (average or above for his time). Mbappé 1.78 m.
- Mbappé: world champion 2018 at 19; France captain since 2023; top speed ~38 km/h (most cited, estimated); Real Madrid's offer in 2021, move in 2024 → "three years of rumours".
- Safety/tone: no guillotine, no violence; the joke is only about Mbappé's well-known traits (speed, celebration, transfer saga). Caricature, not a photo.
